Role Purpose/background

The Website Manager is responsible for coordinating, executing, and optimizing owned websites across priority North American brands, portfolio platforms, and Brand Home digital experiences.

This role ensures website updates, campaign activations, content improvements, and go-live activities are delivered efficiently, accurately, compliantly, and in alignment with global standards, while also reflecting North American brand priorities, shopper/consumer needs, search behavior, and market requirements.

Global teams will set enterprise website standards and platforms, templates, governance, and technical frameworks. This role is responsible for applying those standards within North America, managing regional execution where relevant, identifying local optimization opportunities, and feeding market needs and recurring issues back to global teams.

The role focuses on website operations, content governance, CMS publishing, QA, accessibility, SEO/GEO readiness, tagging, performance monitoring, stakeholder coordination, and continuous improvement of owned digital experiences.

Key responsibilities

    Manage intake, prioritization, and execution of website and platform activation requests from brands/markets, balancing business priorities, launch timing, and compliance requirements
    Publish and update content across websites and portfolio platforms using approved workflows and templates, ensuring content is accurate, accessible, search-ready, and fit for purpose
    Coordinate website updates, campaign activations, content refreshes, and go-live activities, including readiness checklists, stakeholder approvals, QA, tagging, redirects, and post-launch monitoring
    Conduct QA, accessibility, SEO/GEO, tagging, compliance and platform readiness checks. Conduct pre- and post-launch QA across content, links, mobile experience, accessibility, SEO/GEO readiness, metadata, tagging, analytics, privacy, compliance, and platform functionality.
    Ensure all activations comply with global governance, legal, privacy and responsible marketing requirements.
    Coordinate with Content teams, agencies and market stakeholders to resolve content and asset gaps.
    Monitor website performance, execution quality and operational issues.
    Escalate recurring platform, functionality or capability needs to global teams.
    Maintain documentation, workflows and operational standards.

    Serve as the North America subject matter expert for owned website execution, content quality, user experience, and market-specific website needs.
    Partner with Brand, eCommerce, Digital Shelf, Retail Media, CRM, Legal/Regulatory, Analytics, IT, agencies, and global website teams to deliver connected owned digital experiences.
    Maintain a North America website content calendar and prioritization process across campaigns, launches, seasonal moments, evergreen updates, compliance needs, and performance-driven optimizations.
    Support website content strategy by helping determine what content belongs on owned websites versus retailer PDPs, retail media landing pages, CRM, social, or other digital touchpoints.
    Use analytics, SEO/GEO insights, accessibility checks, and stakeholder feedback to identify optimization opportunities and recommend content, UX, or operational improvements.
    Partner with global teams and agencies to identify reusable templates, components, modules, and content patterns that improve speed, consistency, accessibility, and quality.
